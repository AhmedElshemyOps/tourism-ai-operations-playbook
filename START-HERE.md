## Before copying a prompt

1. Identify the accountable human owner.
2. Remove unnecessary personal or confidential data.
3. Replace every placeholder with confirmed information.
4. List approved sources of truth.
5. Tell the AI to expose missing or contradictory information.
6. Require separation of facts, assumptions and recommendations.
7. Verify changing tourism information before use.
8. Record the final human approval.

## Important

AI can structure, analyse, draft and question information. It cannot independently confirm live availability, commercial authority, legal obligations, guest safety or operational readiness. Human accountability remains essential.
