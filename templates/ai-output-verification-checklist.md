# AI Output Verification Checklist

- [ ] All placeholders were replaced.
- [ ] Guest and employee data was minimised.
- [ ] Supplier, rate and contract information came from approved sources.
- [ ] Opening hours, prices, schedules, weather and availability were rechecked.
- [ ] Transfer times and route assumptions were reviewed operationally.
- [ ] Accessibility statements were verified with the provider or official source.
- [ ] Cultural and language tone was reviewed.
- [ ] Facts are separated from assumptions and recommendations.
- [ ] Unresolved items have owners and deadlines.
- [ ] The accountable professional approved the final output.
