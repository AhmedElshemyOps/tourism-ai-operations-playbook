# Prompt Quality Checklist

## Purpose and ownership
- [ ] The business task is specific.
- [ ] The accountable human owner is named by role.
- [ ] The intended tourism user and guest/client context are clear.

## Inputs
- [ ] Required inputs are listed.
- [ ] Sources of truth are identified.
- [ ] Missing, contradictory or unconfirmed information must be exposed.
- [ ] Unnecessary personal and confidential data is excluded.

## Output
- [ ] The output format is defined.
- [ ] Facts, assumptions, recommendations and pending items are separated.
- [ ] Dynamic facts require a source and verification date.

## Controls
- [ ] The AI is prohibited from inventing operational confirmations.
- [ ] Privacy and commercial-confidentiality rules are included.
- [ ] Verification steps are explicit.
- [ ] Final human approval is required.
