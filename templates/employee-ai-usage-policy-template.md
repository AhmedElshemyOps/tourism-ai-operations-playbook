# Employee AI Usage Policy Template

## Approved purpose
Employees may use approved AI tools to structure, summarise, analyse and draft tourism work within their authority.

## Prohibited information
Do not upload unnecessary personal data, payment information, credentials, confidential rates, contracts or restricted company documents.

## Verification
AI output is never an operational confirmation. Employees must verify dynamic facts and obtain approval from the accountable role.

## Prohibited decisions
AI must not independently approve refunds, contracts, rates, safety decisions, supplier commitments, employee actions or legal conclusions.

## Records and incidents
Use approved prompt versions, retain required review records, and report harmful or incorrect outputs through the company incident process.

This template requires adaptation and qualified legal, privacy, cybersecurity and HR review where applicable.
