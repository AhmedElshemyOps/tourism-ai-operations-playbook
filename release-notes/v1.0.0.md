# Tourism AI Operations Playbook v1.0.0

The first public edition of the Tourism AI Operations Playbook provides a structured, responsible AI resource for modern tourism teams.

## Included

- 10 complete professional playbook chapters
- 100 individually documented copy-ready prompts
- DMC operations, tour guiding, transport, product, itineraries, sales, guest experience, quality and governance toolkits
- Human-verification, privacy and missing-information controls
- JSON and CSV prompt indexes
- Training, governance and incident templates
- Fictional implementation scenarios
- GitHub Pages web edition
- Contribution and automated quality workflows

## Responsible use

AI-generated tourism information must be verified through approved or official sources. Do not upload unnecessary guest, employee, supplier, commercial or security information into public AI tools. Human approval remains required before operational use.
