# Fictional Example — Tour Product Review

> All commercial and operational details are fictional.

A half-day city tour receives strong guide feedback but repeated comments about rushed timing and limited family comfort. The product manager has verified duration, route, supplier conditions and guest-feedback themes.

Use the existing-product review prompt to separate evidence from assumptions, identify product gaps and propose testable improvements. Any change to price, inclusions, operating time or supplier commitment requires commercial and operational approval.
