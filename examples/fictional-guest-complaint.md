# Fictional Example — Guest Complaint and Service Recovery

> This is a fictional training scenario. Do not use real guest data in public tools.

A family reports that their pickup arrived 35 minutes late and that updates were unclear. The operations log confirms a vehicle delay but does not confirm the reason.

Use the complaint-response and service-recovery prompts to create an empathetic draft, identify missing facts, propose proportionate recovery options for management review, and define follow-up actions.

The final response must be approved by the authorised service owner. The AI must not promise compensation or assign blame without authority and evidence.
