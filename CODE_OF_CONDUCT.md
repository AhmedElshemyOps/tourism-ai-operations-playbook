# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Contributors must communicate respectfully, focus on professional improvement and recognise the international and multicultural nature of tourism.

Acceptable participation includes constructive feedback, evidence-based corrections, respect for different professional backgrounds and clear disclosure of assumptions.

Unacceptable participation includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, publishing private information, commercial spam, deliberate misinformation or pressure to weaken privacy and safety controls.

## Enforcement

Concerns should be reported privately to the repository maintainer through the professional contact channels listed on https://ahmedqualityops.com/. The maintainer may edit, hide or remove contributions and restrict participation when necessary to protect the project and its community.
